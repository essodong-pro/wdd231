Resource Attributions

This page documents the external image assets, text content definitions, and inspiration utilized across the development of the Sika Togo cultural project platform.

1. Gallery Media Assets (15 Items)

  • Ewe Kente Cloth (ewe-kente.webp): Visual representation referenced from traditional West African textile heritage public domains and historic artisanal weaving archives.
  • Épé-Ékpé Festival (epe-ekpe.webp): Media references adapted from public journalistic coverage and cultural documentation of the Guin people's ancestral stone ceremony.
  • The Tata Somba (tatas-tamberma.webp): Source inspiration provided via open-source educational media networks and UNESCO World Heritage public galleries documenting Koutammakou, the Land of the Batammariba.
  • Akodésséwa Craftsmanship (akodessewa.webp): Photographic references adapted from regional travel and educational documentation of Lomé regional practices.
  • Kloto Pottery (poterie-kloto.webp): Media representation sourced from local artisanal cooperative networks in the Plateaux region.
  • The Gongon Drum (gongon.webp): Photographic assets representing West African percussion traditions across local ethnomusicology archives.
  • Odon-Itsu Festival (odon-itsu.webp): Visual reference capturing regional agricultural harvest festivals and ancestral traditions.
  • The Kamou Dance (kamou.webp): Media asset representing Kabyè culture and ceremonial dance choreography profiles.
  • Tem Weaving of Sokodé (tissage-tem.webp): Photographic references from regional textile conservation projects in Central Togo.
  • Togolese Bogolan Art (bogolan.webp): Visual representations of contemporary organic canvas dyeing and West African design practices.
  • Statuary Art of Togoville (togoville.webp): Media references documenting historical public monuments and regional bas-relief shrines.
  • Legba Statuettes (legba.webp): Photographic records of traditional protective wood and clay sculpture designs.
  • Adewon Dance (adewon.webp): Media assets capturing the ritual hunter dances of the Maritime and Plateaux regions.
  • Contemporary Togolese Painting (peinture-moderne.webp): Visual examples of modern urban art movements and pigment techniques from Lomé galleries.
  • Agbogbo-Za Festival (agbogboza.webp): Media references adapted from cultural event coverages documenting the historic Ewe diaspora migration from Notsé.
  • Site Logo (logo.webp): Custom vector design created to symbolize national emblems and traditional Togolese artistic identities.
